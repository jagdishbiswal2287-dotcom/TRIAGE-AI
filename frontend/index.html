      📚 Clinical Triage Prioritization Protocols
      
          ESI / Manchester Aligned
        
      
        
      
          
      Category 1: Immediate / Resuscitation (Red)
      
          
      
            Immediate life-threat or critical instability. Requires zero wait time and immediate transfer to resuscitation or qualified emergency physician. 
            Criteria include: severe hypoxia (SpO2 < 90%), extreme bradycardia (<45 bpm) or severe tachycardia (>140 bpm), hypotensive collapse (SBP < 85 mmHg), severe respiratory distress (>32/min), active convulsions, or crushing chest pain radiating to arm/jaw with cold diaphoresis.
          
      

          
      Category 2: Very Urgent / High Risk (Orange)
      
          
      
            High-risk presentations, severe pain, or significantly altered vital signs. Target review within 10–15 minutes.
            Criteria include: acute asthma exacerbation with audible wheezing, infant high fever (>100.4°F in <2yo), severe hypertension (SBP ≥ 190 or DBP ≥ 120), persistent vomiting with dehydration signs, or sudden worst headache of life.
          
      

          
      Category 3: Urgent / Moderate Risk (Yellow)
      
          
      
            Moderate acute discomfort with stable vital signs. Target review within 30–60 minutes.
            Criteria include: localized abdominal pain without signs of shock, moderate fever with normal vitals, suspected minor fractures, productive cough without hypoxia.
          
      

          
      Category 4: Standard / Routine (Green)
      
          
      
            Mild, non-urgent, or chronic medical conditions with completely stable vital signs. Suitable for standard outpatient consultation.
            Criteria include: mild seasonal cold, superficial abrasions, routine repeat prescriptions.
          
      

          
      
            Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: TRIAGE-AI provides intake structuring and preliminary severity grouping. A human triage nurse or doctor retains complete authority to adjust priority categories based on clinical bedside evaluation.
